## Interpretation Notes

### Structural Analysis
- **Fault Type**: Thrust fault with reverse movement
- **Movement Direction**: Top-to-southeast
- **Deformation Style**: Ductile-brittle transition
- **Strain Localization**: Concentrated in 2-3m wide zone

### Metamorphic Conditions
- **Temperature**: Medium-grade metamorphism
- **Pressure**: Moderate to high
- **Deformation**: Progressive strain accumulation
- **Recrystallization**: Dynamic recrystallization of quartz

### Weathering Processes
- **Chemical Alteration**: Feldspar to clay minerals
- **Oxidation**: Iron-bearing minerals oxidized
- **Physical Disintegration**: Rock-to-soil transition
- **Profile Development**: Mature saprolite formation
