## Citation Format

This bibliography follows the American Geological Institute citation style. All references are current as of 2022-2023, with preference given to the most recent and authoritative sources.

## Notes

1. Where multiple editions exist, the most recent edition is cited.
2. URLs are provided for digital resources and databases.
3. DOI numbers are included where available for journal articles.
4. Technical report numbers are included for government publications.
