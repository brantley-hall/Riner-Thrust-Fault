Discover the Geological History Beneath Your Land

Explore the ancient thrust fault that shaped the landscape of Riner, Virginia. This interactive investigation combines geological data, mapping, and field observations to understand the complex geological history of your property.

🗺️ Fault Location & Mapping

The thrust fault runs approximately through the central portion of the property, trending northeast-southwest. This geological feature represents ancient tectonic activity that occurred millions of years ago during the Appalachian orogeny.

🔬 Geological Data

⏰ Geological Timeline

Precambrian (>540 million years ago)

Original metamorphic basement rocks formed through regional metamorphism.

Paleozoic Era (540-250 million years ago)

Sedimentary layers deposited over the metamorphic basement.

Appalachian Orogeny (~300 million years ago)

Major tectonic event created the thrust fault through intense compression.

Present Day

Erosion has exposed the fault zone, creating the current landscape.

🔍 Research & Documentation

This geological investigation is part of an ongoing effort to document and understand the geological features of the Riner area. The thrust fault represents a significant geological feature that provides insights into the ancient tectonic history of the Appalachian Mountains.

Field work includes geological mapping, rock sampling, and structural analysis to better understand the fault's characteristics and its relationship to regional geology.
